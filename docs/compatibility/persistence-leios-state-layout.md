# Persistence: the Leios build's `state` layout

Part of the [compatibility matrix](../compatibility.md); the register entry is `persistence-leios-state-layout` in [compatibility.json](../data/compatibility.json).

## Status as recorded

partial: the `state` file of the build `--reference-build leios-b004fe0c` names (cardano-node b004fe0c, cardano-ledger 9df9f98c, ouroboros-consensus 2416493b) is read and written in every Shelley-based era up to Conway (#826 step b) and in Dijkstra (#826 step d): a telescope in Dijkstra, its `NewEpochState` at version 12, the header state's Dijkstra tip and its Praos2 chain-dependent state (`PolyPraos.hs:368-419`). It differs from the 11.1 layout in every such era: the extended state has the Peras state third, read with the default when it has two parts and always written with three (`Ledger/Extended.hs:420-505`); a hard-fork bound has the next Peras round fourth, read as zero from three items and always written with four (`HardFork/History/Summary.hs:573-601`); the Shelley ledger state's fourth field is the cumulative transaction bytes (`Shelley/Ledger/Ledger.hs:885-922`); the `NewEpochState` has no pool distribution (`Shelley/LedgerState/Types.hs:393-422`); the mark, set and go snapshots are wrapped with the mark's epoch, committee size and key age and the set's Leios committee (`State/SnapShots.hs:386-594`); and a pool's state and its stake snapshot have its voting key after the VRF key hash (`State/StakePool.hs:165-266`, `State/SnapShots.hs:280-315`). Each of these records holds a part per layout (a closed enum per `StateLayout`), and each layout's codec writes only its own: a value of one layout is refused by the other's writer, and nothing is converted. `import-ledger-snapshot` and `export-ledger-snapshot` take the build; `follow` refuses it (`ReferenceBuildNotYet.Rules`) until its rules are implemented (step c).

## Evidence

`LeiosLayoutSuite` (storage): property laws of each record of the Leios layout and of a whole `state` file in every era from Shelley to Conway, with valid BLS12-381 voting keys; the refusal of each layout's values by the other's writer, naming where; hand-derived bytes of the bound, the Peras state (this build's constant `82 83 01 80 80 80`, an error state, and an epoch context refused as the reference refuses it before Dijkstra), a voting key that is not a point, and a chunked byte string below version 12; and the captured states of `fixtures/leios/state`: the last Conway state (slot 195) read and written byte for byte and refused by the 11.1 layout's two-part check (82ecba32 `Ledger/Extended.hs:268-280`), a two-part form of it read with the default Peras state and written as captured, and two Dijkstra states read and written byte for byte as whole files: the devnet's first (slot 203) and the public testnet's at slot 2,168,639 (`fixtures/leios/state/testnet-2168639`), whose Praos2 state holds an announcement; the 11.1 layout refuses them at the extended state's length, and their two-part form at the telescope and the tip (`DijkstraNotYet.LedgerState`). `LeiosLayoutImportSuite` (node): slot 195 imported under `leios-b004fe0c` and exported with `state`, `tables` and `meta` byte for byte, an export under `11.1` refused, and the import under `11.1` refused with `Error.State` and nothing published; slot 203 the same, its header's tip moved to the first Dijkstra block of `fixtures/dijkstra-devnet` and its `meta` checksum recomputed, since the block of slot 203 was not kept with the capture and the import checks the snapshot's point against the database's block. `DijkstraEpochStateSuite` (storage): slot 203's `NewEpochState` read and written byte for byte at version 12, and the reference's decoders' switches at version 12 on hand-built items: bounded ratios of `Word64`s, a pool's ten-item parameters, a proposal's update read at 12, and indefinite records and byte strings. `LeiosTxBytesSuite` (node): the cumulative transaction bytes through the mirror's block step on the devnet's slot-195 block with constructed transactions, from zero at the origin, each transaction counted valid or not, wrapping at 2^64, a mirror of the other layout refused, and the total written to and read back from a V10 snapshot; no captured chain of this layout holds transactions, so the total is not compared with the reference's. `ReferenceBuildSuite` and `ReferenceBuildStartSuite`: both layouts have a codec, and `follow` refuses the Leios build at start. `LedgerFileSuite` and `HaskellStateCodecSuite` keep the 11.1 layout's laws and fixtures. The `leios-state`, `leios-peras-state` and `leios-bound` fuzz targets.

Consensus framing is checked in both layouts: `LeiosLayoutSuite` mutates each shared envelope,
including the stable Praos and TPraos state, to an indefinite array and requires refusal. The
stable reference uses `decodeListLenOf`/`decodeListLen`/`enforceSize` (ouroboros-consensus
`82ecba32`, `Ledger/Extended.hs:277`, `HardFork/History/Summary.hs:541`,
`Util/Versioned.hs:176`). Positive controls keep indefinite ledger records accepted; ledger
`decodeRecordNamedT` permits those (cardano-ledger `f649f975`, `Binary/Decoding/Decoder.hs:700-708`).
The Peras framing mutations apply only to Leios, whose extended state includes that component.

`PoolDistributionReferenceSuite` compares the native distributions and individual-pool encodings
in [the two-build corpus](../../fixtures/reference/pool-distribution/README.md), including voting
keys and the omission of a missing fourth field. Every accepted byte and every acceptance verdict
is compared; native error wording is retained but not compared to Scala's typed errors. The suite
also verifies reuse of the distribution for one immutable snapshot and fresh derivation after a
copy changes its pools or active stake. The `leios-pool-distribution` fuzz target exercises the
optional-field decoder. This does not establish agreement for lifecycle rules or committee selection.

`LeiosCommitteeReferenceSuite` compares the Leios committee that a mark snapshot memoizes, and the
set snapshot it rotates into, with the native `mkMarkSnapShot` and `mkSetSnapShot` in
[the Leios corpus](../../fixtures/reference/leios-committee/README.md). The cases cover the size
and key-age limits, unsigned stake order with pool-id ties, keyless seats, the key-age boundaries,
and the `Word64` wrap of the key-age sum and of the mark epoch. Every accepted byte and every
acceptance verdict is compared. `LeiosCommitteeSelection` gives a typed defect, never a keyless
seat, for states the reference cannot hold: a seated pool of the 11.1 layout, an undecodable seated
key, a parameter outside its word, or a failure of the native BLS library.

## Explicitly unchecked or unconfirmed

An epoch context of the Peras state with Dijkstra's voting committee (`V1.PerasVotingCommitteeScheme`, the WFALS committee: `Shelley/Node/Peras.hs:226-234`, `Peras/Voting/V1.hs:39`, `Peras/Context.hs:170-203`) is refused (`DijkstraNotYet.PerasEpochContext`): Peras is off in the build, and no state it wrote holds one. A Dijkstra state is read and written whole, but for a pool's BLS key in its `StakePoolParams` (`DijkstraNotYet.PoolParamsBlsKey`; a pool is written with nine items, as the reference writes one without a key) and a governance proposal that updates a parameter Dijkstra adds (`DijkstraNotYet.ParameterUpdate`). The commands cannot import a Dijkstra snapshot of a real database yet: `import-ledger-snapshot` checks the snapshot's point against a block of the database, which `import-haskell-db` does not store for Dijkstra (`HaskellImportError.EraNotYet` with `DijkstraNotYet.Rules`); Dijkstra's genesis injection is implemented at the ledger level (`Dijkstra/Transition.hs:59-101`), and the node starts the public testnet's chain in Dijkstra as the build does (`DijkstraGenesisStartSuite`). Build selection reaches the bootstrap-attribute failure rule through `LedgerGlobals.referenceBuild`, `TxEnv.referenceBuild` and `UtxoTests.injectedBootAddrAttrsTooBig`, and Conway HARDFORK's count of the VRF key hashes through `LedgerGlobals.referenceBuild` and `HardFork.populateVRFKeyHashes` (recomputed from nothing, as 9df9f98c's `State/CertState.hs:528-538` does); it does not yet select the complete state lifecycle. `Snap` rotates the Leios layout as the build's SNAP does before Dijkstra (ledger 9df9f98c `Shelley/Rules/Snap.hs:75-102`): the new mark records the epoch the chain enters with a committee size and key age of zero, and the old mark's committee (`LeiosCommitteeSelection`) rotates into the set position. A committee SNAP cannot select is no verdict on the block: the node stops (`ChainError.LeiosCommittee`, or a provider defect for a failing BLS library). Dijkstra's SNAP seats a committee of the parameters' size with the key age `maxKeyAgeEpochs` derives from the KES constants (`Dijkstra/Rules/Snap.hs:105-179`), after Dijkstra's EPOCH has enacted the new parameters (`Dijkstra/Rules/Epoch.hs:128-244`); `DijkstraEpochReferenceSuite` compares Dijkstra's tick with the build's own on the captured Dijkstra states. The genesis state of a chain that starts in a Shelley-based era is built in the build's layout: the empty snapshots and the new-epoch state of the Leios layout, each genesis pool's voting key read from its `blsKey` (ledger 9df9f98c `State/StakePool.hs:517-530`, `604-619`) and registered in the state's epoch, the stake distribution seeded into mark, set and go (`Shelley/Transition.hs:598-618`), and the consensus parts of the origin (no transaction bytes, next Peras round zero, a Peras state with neither an epoch context nor a certificate). `LeiosGenesisReferenceSuite` compares Conway's injection with the native `injectIntoTestState` on [the genesis corpus](../../fixtures/reference/leios-genesis/README.md); the Byron to Conway translations before it are not compared on reference bytes. Up to Conway the epoch boundary keeps the Leios layout: NEWEPOCH writes no pool distribution (the build's new-epoch state has none, `Conway/Rules/NewEpoch.hs:153-184`), and a pool that POOL registers or POOLREAP re-registers holds no voting key, since parameters read one from protocol version 12 only and an era's blocks are read at its lowest version (`State/StakePool.hs:746-762`, `Core/Era.hs:259-260`). `LeiosEpochReferenceSuite` compares Conway's tick with the native TICK across three boundaries on [the epoch corpus](../../fixtures/reference/leios-epoch/README.md), with seeded re-registrations and retirements; POOL is unit-tested only. A block's Shelley ledger state carries the cumulative transaction bytes: the block adds the `sizeTxF` of every transaction of its body, valid or not, modulo 2^64 (`Shelley/Ledger/Ledger.hs:749-756`), to the total of the state before it, whatever its era, or to zero when the chain leaves Byron. The Byron to Shelley hand-over's other consensus parts (the Peras state and the bounds of a chain that starts in Byron), and the bound of an era entered after the chain's start, still need the Leios layout behavior, the bytes of an endorser block's transactions applied with its certificate are Dijkstra's (`Shelley/Ledger/Leios.hs:216-229`), and `follow` retains `ReferenceBuildNotYet.Rules`. The layout was diffed against the pinned sources era by era; Conway and Dijkstra are checked on reference bytes.
