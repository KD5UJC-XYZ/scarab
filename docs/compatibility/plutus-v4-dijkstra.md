# Plutus at protocol 12: PlutusV4 and case on Data (#54, #1099)

Part of the [compatibility matrix](../compatibility.md); the register entry is `plutus-v4-dijkstra` in [compatibility.json](../data/compatibility.json). The sections below record the implementation, evidence and remaining limits.

## Status as recorded

partial: the Dijkstra the node implements is the Leios testnet build's (cardano-node `b004fe0c`, cardano-ledger `9df9f98c`, [decisions](../decisions.md), #711), whose node links plutus-core and plutus-ledger-api 1.71.0.0 (`261766ad`), the plutus cardano-node 11.2.1 links too. Scarab's own evaluator (`uplc`, [ADR 0027 and its amendment of 2026-11-15](../adr/0027-own-uplc-evaluator-as-a-correctness-backend.md#amendment-2026-11-15-plutus-17100-and-protocol-12)) implements that plutus at every protocol version (#1099). From protocol 12 it makes program version 1.2.0 available in every language, lets a 1.2.0 program `case` on `Data` (`defaultCaserBuiltinFor`, which reads the program's version), and evaluates PlutusV1 and PlutusV2 in builtin semantics variant F and PlutusV3 and PlutusV4 in G, which drop the integer unlifting bounds of D and E and keep those on byte strings (`ensurableInteger` and `ensurableByteString`; [`SemanticsVariant`](../../uplc/src/main/scala/xyz/kd5ujc/scarab/uplc/SemanticsVariant.scala), [`CoreVariant`](../../uplc/src/main/scala/xyz/kd5ujc/scarab/uplc/builtin/core/CoreVariant.scala)). F has D's cost model and G has E's. Every protocol from 12 on is evaluated as 12, as plutus's functions are total in the protocol version. cardano-ledger `9df9f98c` runs a PlutusV4 script (language 3, hash tag 4) through plutus's PlutusV3 functions, cost model parameter names and evaluation context, and decodes it from protocol 12 only; the own evaluator does the same ([`PlutusLanguage.evaluatedAs`](../../uplc/src/main/scala/xyz/kd5ujc/scarab/uplc/PlutusLanguage.scala)). Its language view is key 3 with a definite list (`ScriptIntegrity.plutusLanguageViews`). `DijkstraScriptContexts` builds the PlutusV4 `ScriptContext` of a Dijkstra transaction and collects a transaction's scripts with their arguments as `mkDijkstraStAnnTopTx` does.

The node does not reach protocol 12: `HardForkConfig.MaxMajorProtVer` is 11 and the envelope check refuses every block once the ledger's major version passes it (`ObsoleteNode`), and the Dijkstra era is not entered.

## Explicitly unchecked or unconfirmed

- The contexts of PlutusV1 to PlutusV3 scripts in a Dijkstra transaction (`guardDijkstraFeaturesForPlutusV1toV3`, the Dijkstra TxInfos of those languages) are not built: they are collected as `Untranslated`. Phase 2 and UTXOW of the Dijkstra era do not use the contexts yet, and `NoCostModel` is the caller's. Of the Leios testnet chain sample's scripts, 239 are PlutusV1 to PlutusV3: `LeiosChainContextsSuite` compares their order, purposes and redeemers with the reference's, not their arguments; their evaluation takes the reference's arguments.
- The Leios testnet chain evidence is a sample (200 of the chain's 107,831 transactions with redeemers; the sampling is in the [fixture README](../../fixtures/reference/leios-testnet-chain-contexts/README.md)), and no transaction of the chain to its tip has a false validity flag: no script that fails on the chain is in evidence.
- The evaluator tags a cost-model list with plutus 1.71.0.0's parameter names at every protocol (351 for PlutusV1 and PlutusV2, 369 for PlutusV3 and PlutusV4), as 1.71.0.0's `mkEvaluationContext` does; the ledger's readers keep 1.70.0.0's tables (361 for PlutusV3 and PlutusV4). No reader of this node depends on the table: a genesis cost model given by name is read by its first 251 names, every other list by position at any length, and the values after the 361st cost only `keepPolicies` and `dropPolicies` (`CostModelNameTablesSuite`). The owner decided on 2026-11-04 that the mainnet lineage reads by the 1.70.0.0 names and the Leios testnet by the 1.71.0.0 names; that rule has no reader to apply to yet, and one that names parameters past the 251st must select the table by the network.
- The generated fixture covers what its generator writes; it is not a proof for every program. The cryptographic builtins' edge cases at protocol 12 rest on the conformance corpus and the denotations' own suites, as at protocol 11.
- The pre-Dijkstra ledger has no PlutusV4 script kind (the persisted UTxO format has none).
