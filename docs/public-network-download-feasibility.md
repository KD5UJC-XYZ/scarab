# Public Cardano download feasibility

Assessed 2026-10-10, against `d57d9796a75c4cbb1e5b31897c259ac7eb296932`. Documentation-only isolated worktree; no public peer connection, chain download, build or devnet modification was performed. Existing fetcher/network documentation and fixture manifests were inspected before proposing work. This remains conditional on successful devnet soak and must not consume its 8–12-hour delivery window.

## Recommendation

Attempt **one historical post-Byron segment of 1–4 blocks**, after the soak, using the existing `chain-fetch-tcp-keepalive` command. This is a small, plausible public-network interoperability demonstration, not an era sync or ledger validation. Budget at most 30 minutes of preparation; if exact points or compatible peers cannot be established within that, report the blocker and stop. No whole-era commitment is justified by the existing implementation.

## What exists and what is missing

- [KeepAlive direct-range profile](keepalive-direct-range.md) and [TCP command](tcp-direct-range.md) implement NtN14, initiator-only, BlockFetch and KeepAlive on one outgoing connection. No transaction submission, listening, peer sharing, automatic retry or topology engine is enabled. Public relay interoperability has not been demonstrated by this path.
- [TcpFetchLimits](../fetcher/src/main/scala/lab/fetcher/TcpDirectRangeDescriptor.scala) and [EndpointBatch](../fetcher/src/main/scala/lab/fetcher/EndpointBatch.scala) already cap four blocks, 1 MiB/block, 4 MiB total raw blocks, 5 MiB received wire data, 16 KiB outgoing bytes, 8 MiB store accounting, 256 files, 30-second acquisition and 45-second invocation. These are real enforced ceilings, not estimates; do not widen them for this demo.
- The command requires a numeric IPv4/IPv6 address, network magic, concrete exclusive predecessor `(slot, hash)`, and inclusive first/last points. It has no DNS resolver, ChainSync discovery or origin mode. Retained fixture documentation identifies a four-block preprod Babbage range with its preceding block; Mary has mainnet source attribution. Actual private range bytes/descriptors were not found in the inspected worktrees, so the point prerequisite was unresolved at the initial assessment; it is now resolved by the [subsequent point verification](public-network-segment-points.md). Other single-era fixtures can be network-unestablished and must not be relabelled mainnet.
- [Post-Byron indexer](post-byron-indexing.md) covers Shelley through Conway structurally. Byron/EBB and null-genesis-parent handling remain unsupported. Starting from chain origin is therefore not a shortcut.

## Official configuration and point provenance

The [Operations Book environment index](https://book.world.dev.cardano.org/environments.html) labels its configuration files official. Its [mainnet topology](https://book.world.dev.cardano.org/environments/mainnet/topology.json) currently lists `backbone.cardano.iog.io:3001` and `backbone.mainnet.cardanofoundation.org:3001` as bootstrap peers. At execution preparation, retrieve/hash the small configuration files, resolve one official DNS name once with a deadline, retain the answer and timestamp, and supply a checked globally routable numeric address. DNS/bootstrap provenance is not cryptographic chain authentication. No peer scan, automatic failover, credentials or firewall changes are needed. [Preprod topology](https://book.world.dev.cardano.org/environments/preprod/topology.json) is an alternative only if its independently verified retained Babbage range is recovered; never reuse mainnet magic or points there.

[Mainnet Shelley genesis](https://book.world.dev.cardano.org/environments/mainnet/shelley-genesis.json) specifies magic `764824073`, one-second slots and 432,000-slot epochs. [Mainnet config](https://book.world.dev.cardano.org/environments/mainnet/config.json) supplies genesis/checkpoint references and hashes; it currently specifies minimum node version 11.1.0. That does not prove an existing local node image is compatible, nor that a relay accepts our NtN14 offer. Do not replace the fetcher with an unattended full-node sync.

[Official checkpoints](https://book.world.dev.cardano.org/environments/mainnet/checkpoints.json) contain block numbers and hashes, **not the required slot/adjacent-range tuple**. For example, block 2000 has hash `a82e2b2253a39897f57912cf8fdd70786eb8a2460c2955bb3521642539b87ead`; it is not a ready post-Byron target. Recover a small independently source-attributed archive range, or obtain slot/hash/parent metadata from a separately trusted historical source and pin it before connecting. Do not invent slots from heights or use a hash fetched from the same peer as independent authentication.

## Whole era versus a segment

An era is not an epoch. The [official hard-fork timeline](https://cardano.org/hardforks/) places Shelley at epoch 208, Allegra at 236 and Mary at 251. Even the relatively short completed Allegra era spans 15 five-day epochs. An illustrative 20-second block interval gives about 324,000 blocks, not four. At assumed average raw sizes of 2–20 KiB this is roughly **0.6–6.2 GiB raw**, before indexes, duplicates, ledger state and logs. These are sensitivity estimates, not measured historical sizes. At an assumed sustained 1–10 MiB/s, raw transfer alone ranges roughly **one minute to two hours**; handshakes, peer throttling, indexing and validation add unmeasured cost. A four-block-per-connection loop could require about 81,000 connections and is not an acceptable whole-era implementation. Byron is longer and unsupported; the current ongoing era has no final endpoint.

The proposed segment instead has a hard 4 MiB raw/5 MiB wire cap and a 45-second invocation ceiling. Data volume is negligible compared with full sync; peer availability and point provenance are the main uncertainties. dev-host showed about 590 GiB free at inspection, but this is not a reservation and must be rechecked after the soak.

## Proposed execution and cleanup contract

1. After soak completion, pin the tested build, profile, official configuration/provenance and exact historical point triple. Use one peer and one attempt. If negotiation, history availability, unsupported traffic or parsing fails, retain the bounded failure; no silent protocol fallback or retries.
2. Run only the downloader with 2 CPU, 2 GiB memory and 256 PIDs, no credentials/signing keys, no listening service. Allow outgoing traffic for that selected peer; do not alter host firewall rules. Protocol request messages are necessary, but there are no public transactions or ledger writes.
3. Keep existing fetch limits above. Add a supervisor total budget of **120 seconds including 30 seconds reserved for owned cleanup**; configuration/DNS preparation has a separate 60-second/1 MiB cap. Cap logs at 2 MiB. Monitor the unique output root every 250 ms for a **32 MiB total ceiling** and **60 GiB free reserve**; stop on breach. Monitoring can overshoot and is not a hard filesystem quota. Include container writable-layer/temp/log usage, or use a read-only root and bounded temp mount.
4. Verify exact range completion through BatchDone, original header hashes, parent links, era tags and observed byte counts. Keep `ledgerValidated=false`, `consensusValidated=false`, and whole-era completion false. Optional body-commitment inspection is separately reported and cannot confer ledger validity.
5. Close/join the downloader and verify owned process/container absence. Resolve and verify the newly created output root is inside the dedicated demo parent, reject symlinks, then remove only that run's raw blocks/store/temp/logs. Never prune Docker or touch other worktrees, images or clusters. Verify deletion. Retain at most **64 KiB** of sanitized summary: source/config hashes, network/profile, block points and byte hashes, counts, timing, stop reason and cleanup result; no raw transaction bodies or host paths.

## Validation boundary and decision

Historical ledger validation needs an authenticated state immediately before the range: UTxO, parameters, accounts/rewards, stake snapshots, pools/delegations, certificates, nonce/eligibility and relevant governance/history, followed by the correct era rules. Genesis files and structural parent links do not supply that state. The current restricted PV9 epoch-zero devnet admission/restore path cannot be applied to arbitrary public historical state. Full reference validation would require independently authenticated historical state or replay of the preceding chain, undermining the lightweight scope.

**Go for a bounded segment only after the soak and point/peer prerequisites pass. Defer a complete era.** If the prerequisites exceed the preparation budget, the successful devnet soak is the appropriate deliverable. This document authorizes no launch and records no public-download success.
