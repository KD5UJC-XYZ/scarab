# Decisions register

**Status: Current.** One row per decision the owner has taken that fixes how Scarab behaves or how its
work is run, with the invariant it fixes and what would reopen it. The record column links where the
decision is written down (an ADR, a design document or the issue comment that recorded it); the record
is authoritative and this register is its index. Indexing changes nothing about a decision's authority: an
explicit owner decision binds whether or not a row cites it yet, and a decision is added here in the change
that first relies on it ([one place per question](process.md#one-place-per-question)). The register was seeded
on 2026-10-29 from the #474, #567, #573 and #584 decision comments, the ADR amendments and ADRs they cite, and
the roadmap's recorded decisions; a decision recorded only elsewhere is added when a change first relies on it.

- **Dates** are the dates the decision was taken, as its record states them. A comment posted later
  (for example after midnight UTC) is linked with its own date.
- **Reopen when** repeats only a condition the record states; "none recorded" means the record names
  none, and changing the decision then needs the owner again.
- **A superseded decision** keeps its row, struck through (`~~…~~`), with the row or amendment that
  replaced it.
- **Labels.** An issue that carries a decision gets the `owner-decision` label;
  [#612](https://github.com/KD5UJC-XYZ/scarab/issues/612) creates the label and has the triage check report a
  labelled issue that no row here cites.
- Wording is neutral: a row states the decision, never a quotation of the owner.
