### Changed (ledger, #474)

- An epoch with no block is no longer crossed boundary by boundary: the tick of a slot runs once, in the epoch of the slot, and NEWEPOCH does nothing for an epoch that is not the next one, as in the reference (`Shelley/Rules/NewEpoch.hs:148`).
- A quantity of the ledger state, its parameters included, outside the range of its vocabulary type stops the node (`ChainError.StateOutOfRange`, a fatal stop) instead of refusing a block or a transaction and blaming the peer, wherever the reference's `compactCoinOrError` throws (cardano-ledger `Coin.hs:184-188`): in the tick of a block, in the deposit conversions of DELEG, POOL and GOVCERT (`LedgerFailure.StateOutOfRange`), in the Byron to Shelley hand-over of the header forecast and of the block, and in the forger, which no longer counts it as a failed slot and tries the next one (ouroboros-consensus `Storage/ChainDB/Impl/Background.hs:107-109`, `NodeKernel.hs:378-380`). Nothing is recorded as invalid. A value taken from the block or the transaction stays a refusal.
- LEDGERS enumerates the transactions of a block with the bounded `TxIx` of the reference (`zip [minBound ..]`, `Shelley/Rules/Ledgers.hs:201-211`): positions 0 to 65,535, and a transaction past them is not applied. The pointer DELEGS gives a certificate has the slot cut to its low 32 bits and the certificate index to 16, as the reference's `SlotNo32` (`Shelley/Rules/Delegs.hs:192-194`, `Credential.hs:233`); it only differs for a slot past 2^32. The persisted format is unchanged.
- The consensus state's per-epoch transition counter passing `Word32` stops the node (`ChainError.StateOutOfRange`) instead of refusing the block and blaming the peer: the reference's `succ` throws there on the block path.
