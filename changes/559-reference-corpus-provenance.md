### Changed (reference corpus provenance, #559)

- **`scripts/check_test_coverage.py` now validates `provenance.json` of every reference corpus.** Required fields, source revision, case counts, corpus hashes and `notCovered` gaps are checked, so a record missing the count of its own cases, carrying a stale digest or naming a gap no register entry tracks fails in preflight and CI with the record and field named, instead of reading as evidence. The rules are stated in `fixtures/README.md`.
- **Nine reference corpora are corrected to the schema, without touching any measured value.** Eight records gain the `schemaVersion` they predate, five of those also the `scope` sentence their README already states; `genesis-initial-funds` records the ledger commit under `ledgerSourceCommit` instead of an ambiguous `reference`; `plutus-boundary-transactions` wraps its four per-batch objects in one record with a scope and the reference revision. No case, verdict, count or digest changed.
- **Five corpus manifests are re-pinned** to the `provenance.json` bytes they name, and `docs/data/fixture-inventory.json` is refreshed for the corrected records.
- **Counts remain required without a standard case file, and malformed values fail.** Batch counts and accepted/refused splits reject negative values, booleans and other invalid types; digest fields reject nulls, numbers and empty containers. Three API records (`auxiliary-scripts`, `context-errors`, `context-matrix`) gain the observation counts already documented and retained in their API rows, with their manifest and inventory pins updated.
