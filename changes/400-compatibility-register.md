### Changed (compatibility register, #400)

- `docs/compatibility.md` is now generated by `scripts/render_compatibility.py` from the register `docs/data/compatibility.json`: a short status table (component, status, one-line summary, evidence and limitation pointers). The long evidence prose moved unchanged to one page per component under `docs/compatibility/`, and the evidence log to `docs/compatibility/evidence-log-N.md`. CI runs the script with `--check`, which also validates the status enum, suite names, paths and anchors; `scripts/check_compat_lossless.py` verified that no issue reference, path, symbol, URL, number or cell was lost.
