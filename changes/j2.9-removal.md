### Removed (ledger-state mirror, step 2, jump 2: the old rules, J2.9, #82)
- **The old rules, the flat state and the differential harness are gone.** `NodeRules.applyFull`, `applyBootstrap`,
  `tickLedger`, `tick` and `OldTick`, `Epoch`, `Stake`, `ConwayRules`, `PreConwayRules`, `TxRules`, `PpupRules`, the flat
  `LedgerState`, `NodeState`, `LedgerMirror.from` and `rebuilt`, `StateMirror.fromMirror`, `FlatEraRead`,
  `ChainRead.nodeState`, `MirrorCheck`, `differential/*`, `Shadowed` and `RuleSet.Old` are removed, and `Main` is the
  era-indexed rules on `MirrorState` alone (no `Mode`, no `UtxoHandle[NodeState]`). About 6,800 lines fewer in all (7,100
  of production code removed, 2,500 added). What the old rules and the new ones shared stays, under the names the new rules
  use: the transaction checks (`TxChecks`, `TxEnv`, `UpdateChecks`, `GovChecks`, `RatifyView`, `PlutusStats`).
- **`--rules`, `--mirror-check`, `--on-divergence` and `--repro-dir` are refused**, each with a typed
  `ConfigProblem.RemovedFlag` that says what to do (exit status 2): a command line that still names one is a mistake worth
  hearing about, not a flag to ignore. Exit status 3 (a halt on a divergence) is no longer used. `SCARAB_RULES`, the
  differential mode of `devnet.py` (`soak --rules differential`, `--mirror-check`) are gone (so is the nightly
  `e2e-differential` job, in the workflow that `ci/nightly-matrix` introduces); `--snapshot-check full` is the read-back check of the state. The events `mirrorChecked`, `mirrorCheckFailed`,
  `differentialSampled`, `differentialTicked`, `differentialSummary`, `rulesDiverged` and `rulesHalted` are removed.

### Changed
- **Snapshots are format 5 (`SCRBSNP5`).** The sidecar lost what only the old rules used (the flat ledger state of a Byron
  chain, `NodeExtras` less `plutus`), so its fingerprint is new (`7c483457b06802d6`, `CodecSuite`) and the version is
  bumped (ADR 0006). A database with snapshots of format 1 to 4 has them rewritten as format 5 the first time this build
  opens it, one file at a time, atomically; a format-4 file is read by the frozen V4 reader of the `legacy` module and the
  old rules' bookkeeping is put right once (`LegacySeed`, kept) before it is dropped. Checked on real files: four V4 files of
  the K1 Preview sync migrate, restore and read back with the full check. Test: `SnapshotV5Suite` (V1, V2, V3 and V4
  migrations, the 15 V3 files of `fixtures/snapshot-v3`, the V4 sidecar's frozen fingerprint). An older build cannot read
  format 5: keep a copy of `<db>/snapshots/` to go back.
- **The `legacy` module** holds the flat `LedgerState`, `FlatNodeState` and `NodeCodecs` of the V2 to V4 readers, the
  conversion of that state to the mirror (`Mirroring`: `LedgerMirror.to`, `StateMirror.toMirror`) and `LegacySeed`; nothing
  but the migration and the genesis start calls it. The rule suites over the flat state (`PreConwayRulesSuite`,
  `ConwayRulesSuite`, `ConwayGovernanceSuite`, `RewardsSuite`, the recorded Preview, Preprod and mainnet cases) keep
  their assertions and run in `legacy/src/test`, on the era-indexed rules through `RuleSet` (now an object); their `Old`
  classes are deleted with the rules they tested.
- **Re-recorded goldens** (`fixtures/projection-golden/eras.tsv`, `fixtures/ntc-golden/conway-answers.tsv`): the recorded text
  was made from the old rules' states. The projection differs from the recording in two things only (the Alonzo genesis'
  parameter fields the reference's `PParams` of an era lacks, and a reward update during the first Shelley epoch that the
  reference makes only at `4k/f`; laying those two over the new text gives back every recorded digest), and the node-to-client
  answers in the arguments drawn from the state and in the two synthetic `rich` states; no answer of the three real Conway
  states that takes no argument from the state changed (`fixtures/ntc-golden/README.md`).

#### Known gaps
- The epoch boundary of the era-indexed rules is slower than the old one: p99 +46% on the largest Preview state (the gate was
  +25%; waived to proceed, `docs/performance.md`). It is once per epoch, about 1% of sync time at mainnet scale; the
  optimisation is [#131](https://github.com/KD5UJC-XYZ/scarab/issues/131).
- The genesis start still builds the flat `GenesisState` and converts it with `Mirroring.toMirror`; a direct mirror start
  removes that and the flat types from the node's start path (follow-up, `docs/design/ledger-mirror-step2.md`).
