### Added (era types, #85)

- The era types collect a transaction's Plutus scripts with their contexts as the reference does: each provided script as a runnable one of its language (`mkSupportedPlutusRunnable`), the scripts needed resolved against them and a cache (`resolveNeededPlutusScriptsWithPurpose`), and each with its redeemer, its language's cost model and its arguments (`mkPlutusWithContext`, `scriptsWithContextFromLedgerTxInfo`, `collectPlutusScriptsWithContext`), failing with the era's `CollectError`. A runnable script holds its binary and its hash, computed when first asked for, its decoding left to the evaluator's capability; a cost model is typed by its language, so a script is given its own language's. The node's own `Undecodable` of an inline datum now keeps the decoder's failure. On the Conway corpus the scripts and failures collected are the existing path's, in order. The node still builds its contexts with the existing code.
