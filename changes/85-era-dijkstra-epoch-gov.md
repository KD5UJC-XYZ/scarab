### Added (era types, #85)

- Dijkstra's epoch boundary governance steps on the era types, in the reference's order: the retiring pools, the ratification the DRep pulser reached, its treasury withdrawals, the proposals with the enacted actions made their trees' roots and the competing and expired ones removed with their descendants, the next epoch's parameters, the removed actions' deposits returned, the dormant epochs counted, the deposits counted again, the hard fork rule, the new snapshot, and a fresh DRep pulser computed to completion with the era's ratification. The future parameters are predicted within an epoch and made definite from the slot of no return as the reference does. Every recorded Dijkstra epoch case of the Leios build gives the build's states byte for byte through these steps. A new reference case of the Leios build proposes a change of the endorser block's execution units and reference-script size with the votes to pass, and the states reached are the build's: the change is ratified at the next boundary and enacted at the one after, or, when no tick passes the slot of no return, removed with the parameters kept, as the reference does. Two more cases of the build remove, at a boundary, an enacted action's competing sibling with its descendant and an expired action with its descendant, keeping the rest in order, and credit a proposal's deposit to both the delegated DRep and the pool of the account it returns to; the states, the proposals kept and removed and the credited stakes are the build's. On private states of the Leios testnet, the pulser each boundary starts is the one the reference stored, including the ratification of that change and its enactment. The node still runs its existing epoch rule, and nothing persisted changes.
