### Fixed (decoding bounds, #749)

- Headers, blocks, transactions and node-to-client queries are decoded with no budget of CBOR items, string bytes or nesting, as the reference's decoders read them; the mini-protocol codecs and the multiplexer bound their bytes and the rules (`maxBHSize`, `maxBBSize`, `maxTxSize`) their sizes after decoding. A Leios header whose issuer key is written as hundreds of empty chunks within `maxBHSize`, and a Byron epoch boundary header with hundreds of attributes, are now read as the reference reads them (`fixtures/reference/header-decoding`).
- LocalTxSubmission reads a transaction of any size, as the reference does, and the ledger judges it by `maxTxSize` instead of the connection ending at 1 MiB; a LocalStateQuery query over 2,000,000 bytes or 200,000 items is answered.
