### Added (era types, #85)

- Dijkstra's certificate rules and ENTITIES run on the era types, as the Leios testnet's build (cardano-ledger 9df9f98c) has them. CERTS, DELEG and GOVCERT are Conway's at Dijkstra, and CERT and POOL are Dijkstra's own. POOL checks a voting key's proof of possession and keeps the count of each VRF key's holders. ENTITIES checks a transaction's withdrawals against the accounts before and after its sub-transactions, its direct deposits and its account balance intervals. Their failures are the reference's own types with its injections. ENTITIES' injections cover exactly the cases it can reach, and those the reference calls impossible do not compile. Of the reference's 20 certificate cases, the 15 refusals encode to the reference's bytes exactly and equal the existing rule's failures, and the 5 acceptances leave the existing rule's state. A devnet pool's recorded voting key registers. The node still judges with the existing rules, so nothing changes in behaviour.
