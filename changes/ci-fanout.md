### Changed (ci)

- CI builds once and fans the Scala tests out. "Scala build, static checks and native guards" runs `checkBuild`, the new alias that is `check` without its tests (scalafmt, the SPDX headers, the uplc class path, every module's test sources compiled, scalafix), with `node/runtimeClasspathFile` and the native-library guards, and uploads its compiled output. Four jobs, "Scala tests, shard 1" to "4" (four, for the free plan's limit of 20 jobs at a time), unpack it and run every module's suites between them without compiling: a module runs whole with the `<module>/Test/test` that `check` runs, and `node`'s suites are spread over the shards with `node/Test/testOnly`. `scripts/ci_test_shards.py` plans the shards from the suites sbt reports (`testSuiteInventory`) and the measured seconds in `scripts/ci_test_shards.json`, refuses a suite in no shard or in two and a shard whose commands would run one of its suites twice, and fails a shard whose sbt log shows a compilation. "Scala tests and native guards" now requires the build and every shard and checks, from their JUnit reports, that every suite has exactly one report, from the shard it was planned in, with no failure or error; "scalafmt, compile and test" requires all of them. `check` itself is unchanged.
