### Changed (ci)

- CI builds once and fans the Scala tests out. "Scala build, static checks and native guards" runs `checkBuild`, the new alias that is `check` without its tests (scalafmt, the SPDX headers, the uplc class path, every module's test sources compiled, scalafix), with `node/runtimeClasspathFile` and the native-library guards, and uploads its compiled output. Six jobs, "Scala tests, shard 1" to "6", unpack it and run every module's suites between them without compiling: a module runs whole with the `<module>/Test/test` that `check` runs, and `node`'s suites are spread over the shards with `node/Test/testOnly`. `scripts/ci_test_shards.py` plans the shards from the suites sbt reports (`testSuiteInventory`) and the measured seconds in `scripts/ci_test_shards.json`, refuses a suite in no shard or in two, and fails a shard whose sbt log shows a compilation. "Scala tests and native guards" now requires the build and every shard and checks, from their JUnit reports, that every suite ran exactly once, in its shard, and passed; "scalafmt, compile and test" requires all of them. `check` itself is unchanged.
