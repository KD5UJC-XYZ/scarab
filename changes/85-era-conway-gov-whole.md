### Added (era types, #85)

- Conway's GOV rule runs whole on the era types: the proposals' checks and ancestry, the votes' checks, the deposits, the treasury withdrawals, the guardrails script hash and expiry, over the reference's store of proposals, read from the node's record of them and written back. Its failures and events are the reference's own types. On every Conway case of the refusal corpora, at the recorded protocol version and at 9, 10 and 11, it gives the existing rule's failures in the same order or the same proposals after; on the upstream governance cases it leaves the reference's proposals. Where the reference refused a transaction for GOV alone, its failures encode to the reference's refusal byte for byte. The node still judges with the existing rule, so nothing changes in behaviour.
