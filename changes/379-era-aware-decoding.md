### Fixed (ledger decoding, #379)

- Read a transaction output and a transaction body as the decoders of its era do. Shelley, Allegra and Mary outputs are the two-element array alone, Alonzo adds the datum-hash form, and the map form is Babbage and later. Each era refuses the body keys it does not know (for example a mint in Shelley, or a Conway key before Conway), Shelley requires the time to live, Allegra reads key 9 as `null`, and a key beyond 32 bits is refused instead of read as its low 32 bits, as is a constructor tag beyond 32 bits of a certificate, a credential, a DRep, a voter or a governance action. Outputs already stored by earlier builds keep reading in every form and with the map keys those builds read, and the original bytes that are hashed are unchanged.
