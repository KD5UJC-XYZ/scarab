### Added (peer selection, G1.1)
- **The outbound peers are chosen by a peer governor that follows the reference** (`Ouroboros.Network.PeerSelection.Governor`):
  every known peer is cold, warm or hot (and in the transient states between), the governor promotes and demotes toward the
  targets (the relay defaults of the reference: 60 root, 150 known, 30 established, 20 active; overridable with `TargetNumberOfRootPeers`,
  `TargetNumberOfKnownPeers`, `TargetNumberOfEstablishedPeers`, `TargetNumberOfActivePeers` in the configuration file), with
  the reference's backoffs, forgetting and pick policies. `--peer` is one local-root group with hot = warm = the number of
  peers, the topology file's local roots honour their `hotValency` and `warmValency`, and `bootstrapPeers` are used with the
  sensitive state (targets drop to zero and the local roots are set aside until the node is caught up; the node stops if it
  has not reached the clean state in 15 minutes, as the reference does).
- **Warm and hot are separate**: a warm connection only keeps alive; ChainSync, BlockFetch and TxSubmission2 start when the
  governor activates the peer and end cleanly (`MsgDone`) when it deactivates it. Block claims belong to the hot session.
- **The Genesis State Machine** (`Ouroboros.Consensus.Node.GSM`, PreSyncing, Syncing, CaughtUp, with the `caught_up.marker`
  file) is ported and the fetch mode (BulkSync or Deadline) is derived from it.
- **Events, metrics and endpoint**: log events `PeerSelectionTargets`, `PeerSelectionRoots`, `PeerSelectionPromote`,
  `PeerSelectionDemote`, `PeerSelectionForget`, `PeerSelectionSensitive`, `PeerSelectionCounters`, `OutboundConnections`,
  `GsmTransition`, `FetchModeChanged`; the 41 `cardano_node_metrics_peerSelection_*_int` gauges of the reference, transition
  counters and the GSM and fetch mode gauges at `/metrics`; `GET /connections` gains `peerSelection`.

### Changed
- A peer that breaks the protocol, sends an invalid header or an oversize message is forgotten (unless it is a local or a
  bootstrap root); a network failure only backs the peer off (`FailurePolicy.classify`).
- The node no longer stops after 30 consecutive outbound failures: the governor keeps trying. The only stop is the
  bootstrap-peers timeout.

### Removed
- `Outbound`, the fixed fan-out over `--peer` and its supervisor, and the log event `Reused`.
