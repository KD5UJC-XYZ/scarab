### Added (pool dashboard and alerts, #117)
- **A "Scarab pool" Grafana dashboard** (`scarab-pool`, generated by `ops/monitoring/server/grafana/build_dashboards.py`): blocks
  forged, adopted and lost against the expected count per epoch, leader slots checked and won, forge failures, KES periods
  remaining, current, start and expiry, the certificate counter, reloads by outcome and persist failures, the rotation
  driver's last success and exit status, the slot lag behind the peers' tip, peers hot, warm and cold, the node phase, the
  stall watchdog and restarts. Every metric already existed; none was added to the node.
- **Pool alert rules** (`ops/monitoring/server/prometheus/rules/pool.yml`, 17 alerts, loaded by the server's Prometheus, tested
  with `promtool test rules`): KES periods below 10 and below 3, the rotation driver stale for 3 hours or failing, an evolved key
  not persisted, a forged block neither adopted nor lost, a lost block, a forge failure, no hot peers, more than 120 slots behind
  the peers for 10 minutes, a stalled follower, a restart, a node that does not answer. Labels are `severity` only; no
  notification target is configured.
- **`rotate_kes.py --metrics-file`** (`ROTATE_KES_METRICS_FILE`) writes a node exporter textfile after each run:
  `scarab_kes_rotation_{last_run,last_success,last_rotation}_timestamp_seconds`, `..._last_exit_status` and
  `..._periods_remaining`. The monitoring agent reads such files from `TEXTFILE_DIR`. See "Pool monitoring" in `docs/running.md`.
- `scripts/tests/test_dashboards.py` checks that the committed dashboard JSON is what the generator writes and that every metric a
  panel or a rule reads exists in the node's catalogue or in the driver's file.
