### Added (era types, #85)

- Conway's refusals can be written from the era-typed failures directly, constructor by constructor as the reference's encoders write them, without the flat form: collection errors, missing redeemers and governance actions that the flat form could not hold without the transaction among them. On every refusal the era rules give in the reference's corpora and upstream judgments they are the reference's bytes, and on every refused corpus case the existing encoder's. The node still sends the existing encoder's bytes, so nothing changes in behaviour.
