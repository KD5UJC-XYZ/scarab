### Changed (the fibers of a peer session end in a fixed order, #383)
- **A peer session cancels the fibers it supervises youngest first, one at a time.** The per-batch fetch and pipeline fibers of a session were supervised by the `Supervisor` of cats-effect, which keys its children by identity and cancels them in parallel, so which in-flight work completed before a session died depended on JVM identity hash codes. `OrderedSupervisor` (network module, next to `Fibers`) keeps the children in an insertion-ordered `Ref` and cancels them youngest first, each awaited before the next, as the registry of the reference does for the threads of a peer (resource-registry 0.3.0.0 `ResourceRegistry.hs` 551 to 553, 650, 735). The contract of `supervise` and the error boundaries of ADR 0017 (`Fault.fiber`, `Fault.land`) are unchanged. `UpstreamGenesisReplaySuite` case `upstream-Uniform-1-seed-335004` passes in 300 of 300 runs under default hashing and in 200 of 200 under each of `-XX:hashCode=0` and `-XX:hashCode=4`; before the change it failed in 37 of 1500, 3 of 300 and 8 of 300.
