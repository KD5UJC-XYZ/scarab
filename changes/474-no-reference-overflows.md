### Changed (overflows, #474)

- A wrap, overflow or underflow of the reference is no longer reproduced on a value from outside the node, as the owner's standing rule says. Every place is recorded, with the reason and the arithmetic of its reach, in `docs/compatibility/overflow-improvements.md`, and `scripts/check_overflow_register.py` keeps the code and that page in step.
- Configuration and genesis: a Byron genesis number outside its field's word (`protocolMagic` from 2^32, `bootStakeholders` and `scriptVersion` from 2^16, a negative `k` or `omega`, a `Word64` string such as a balance from 2^64 or below 0) is refused with a typed problem naming the field and its range, where the reference took it modulo 2^bits. A Shelley genesis whose doubled stability window `2 ⌈3k/f⌉` reaches 2^64, and a `PBftSignatureThreshold` whose window threshold leaves `0 .. 2^64 - 1`, are refused. A JSON number's exponent is exact, so a value whose exponent the reference's machine `Int` would wrap is out of range for its field.
- Ledger and consensus: compact coin sums (balances, stake, deposits, rewards, the DRep pulser), the instant stake, the Byron linear fee's scaled size, the PBFT window threshold, the pointer a registration stores before Conway, a genesis delegation's due slot and the Leios header rules compute exactly, and a KES window whose end reaches 2^64 is refused, as the reference's wrapped window refuses it; where the result does not fit the reference's type, the block has no verdict instead of the reference's wrapped one. No public network reaches any of these.
- Unchanged, and recorded: the after-voting deadline's subtraction (a private network's genesis reaches it), the Byron header slot, a pointer address's natural past 64 bits, the Byron fee summand at decode and the SCLS DRep expiry.
