### Fixed (ledger, #734)

- Every field of the Byron genesis is read as the reference's `FromJSON GenesisData` reads it, in its order, whichever era the chain starts in: `bootStakeholders` values must be numbers and keys 28-byte hashes, `heavyDelegation` is required and each key must be its issuer's hash with no delegate an issuer, delegation keys are padded canonical base64 of 64 bytes, `startTime` is required, `k` and `protocolMagic` are taken modulo 2^64 and 2^32, and each `blockVersionData` field is read with its own type (sizes as `Natural`, thresholds as `Word64` of at most 10^15, the fee multiplier as `Integer`, numbers in strings only except `scriptVersion`). A Byron genesis the reference refuses no longer starts a node whose chain begins in Shelley.
