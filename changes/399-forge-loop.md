### Changed (block production, #399)

- The forging loop follows the reference's `forkBlockForging` step by step and traces each step as it does, with the reference's names, severities and machine fields: `startLeadershipCheck`, `blockContext`, `ledgerState`, `noLedgerView`, `ledgerView`, `forgeStateUpdateError`, `nodeCannotForge`, `nodeNotLeader`, `nodeIsLeader`, `forgeTickedLedgerState`, `forgingMempoolSnapshot`, `forgedBlock`, `didntAdoptBlock`, `forgedInvalidBlock`, `adoptedBlock` (namespace `Forge.*`), and `stateInfo` (`Forge.StateInfo`) after each update of the forge state. The `forgeSkipped` event is removed.
- The forger no longer waits for a peer: like the reference it forges on its own chain. It runs a slot only once the slot is known, as the reference's `knownSlotWatcher` gets it: after the system start, and while the era history at the tip of the chain reaches the wall clock's slot; otherwise it asks again after 60 seconds, and nothing is logged or counted. A slot that passes while an earlier one is still being forged is passed over without an event; `busy` and `notConnected` are no longer counted.
- The leader check takes the pool distribution of the forecast ledger view, as the header rules do: into the epoch after the predecessor's the mark snapshot, two or more epochs on the predecessor's current distribution (TICKF), not that of the ledger ticked across the boundaries.
- A slot whose ledger view cannot be forecast from the predecessor of the block is `noLedgerView` (was `forgeSkipped` with the reason `tipTooOld`), and the bound is the reference's: every slot before the stability window after the predecessor's successor, so at genesis the window itself is excluded (it was included).
- The KES key evolves when the forge state is updated, after the ledger view is known and before the leader check, and no longer at every slot. A key past its certificate's window is poisoned, as in the reference: every slot then logs `forgeStateUpdateError` (`Critical`) until a reload installs a new key. A leader slot with a certificate whose window has not begun is `nodeCannotForge` (`PraosCannotForgeKeyNotUsableYet`); both were `forgeFailed` with `KeyPeriodOutOfRange` in slots the pool led.
- The leader check is on the stake alone: the VRF key is no longer compared with the registered one first. A block forged with a VRF key that is not the pool's is refused by our own rules (`forgedInvalidBlock`), as in the reference. A block our rules refuse removes its transactions from the mempool and ends the slot; no second block without transactions is tried.
- The block's transactions are the longest prefix of the still-valid held transactions whose measures (size plus 4 bytes, execution units, reference-script bytes) fit the block's capacity (the body size less 1024 bytes), as the reference's `snapshotTake` takes them: the first transaction that does not fit ends the selection, where a later, smaller one was taken before.
- Metrics: the `cardano_node_metrics_Forge_*` counters count the traces of the same name (`Forge_about_to_lead_counter` every slot the loop starts, `Forge_node_is_leader_counter` only leader slots that can forge, `Forge_node_not_leader_counter` only `nodeNotLeader`, `Forge_didnt_adopt_counter` only slot battles lost at adoption). New: `cardano_node_metrics_Forge_slot_is_immutable_counter`, `cardano_node_metrics_Forge_block_from_future_counter`, `cardano_node_metrics_Forge_could_not_forge_counter` (`noLedgerView` and `nodeCannotForge`), `cardano_node_metrics_Forge_forged_invalid_counter`, the `cardano_node_metrics_Forge_StateUpdateError_int` gauge (the slot of the last failed forge-state update), `scarab_forge_replaced_total` (adopted blocks replaced later) and `scarab_forge_failed_total` (refusals of Scarab's own). `nodeCannotForge_int` counts only `nodeCannotForge`, and `nodeIsLeader_int`, `blocksForged_int` and `slotsMissed_int` are the reference's `ForgingStats`, written at the start of each slot with its count of missed slots. The KES gauges are written at each update of the forge state, and zeroed by a failed one. The `reason` label of `scarab_forge_skipped_total` is `SlotIsImmutable`, `BlockFromFuture`, `NoLedgerView` or `Other`.
- `GET /forging` and `forgeEpoch` add `notLeader`, `cannotForge`, `stateUpdateError`, `forgedInvalid` and `replaced`; `lostSlotBattles` counts only `didntAdoptBlock`, and `skippedByReason` has the keys `slotIsImmutable`, `blockFromFuture` and `noLedgerView`.
- The slot clock checks each reading against the previous one as the reference's blockchain time does: a clock moved back by 20 seconds or less is traced (`systemClockMovedBack`, `BlockchainTime.SystemClockMovedBack`) and the slot kept; moved back further, the node stops (`ClockProblem.SystemClockMovedBack`), where it waited before. The wait for the system start and an unknown slot are traced (`startTimeInTheFuture`, `currentSlotUnknown`, `BlockchainTime.*`, `Warning`).
- By default the forging loop's lines are written from `Info` up, as cardano-node's default configuration sets `Forge.Loop`; `--log-severity Forge=Debug` writes its `Debug` traces again.
