### Added (peer sharing, protocol, #138)
- **The PeerSharing mini-protocol (node-to-node number 10) is spoken**, client and server, from the reference's CDDL
  (`peer-sharing-v14.cddl`): `MsgShareRequest`, `MsgSharePeers` and `MsgDone`, IPv4 and IPv6 peer addresses, the 5760-byte
  message limit, the 60 s limit of the busy state, and an answer with more peers than asked for is a protocol error. The codec has
  typed decode errors, a fuzz target (`peersharing`) and the CDDL as a root of the models cross-check.
- **The handshake carries the flag**: with `PeerSharing: true` in the configuration the node offers peer sharing, and a
  connection takes part in it when both sides offered it. The established temperature of such a connection has the client; the
  server answers the peer's requests. The server policy (the peers advertised, connected successfully and without failures, the
  salt held for 823 s, at most 10 per answer) is a pure function, `PeerShare`.
- Not yet used by the node: the governor does not ask for peers and the server answers with an empty list until the governor
  supplies the shareable peers (G1.4b), so `PeerSharing: true` is still listed in `configUnsupported`.
- The devnet `relay` scenario turns peer sharing on for the Scala node (`--no-peer-sharing` leaves it off).
