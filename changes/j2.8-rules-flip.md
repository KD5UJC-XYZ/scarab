### Changed (ledger-state mirror, step 2, jump 2: the default rules are the era-indexed ones, J2.8, #82)
- **`--rules new` is the default, and the chain's state is the mirror.** `follow` runs the era-indexed rules on
  `VolatileChain[MirrorState]`: a block is ticked by the hard-fork combinator's rules, its header validated against the
  ticked state (the leader check against `nesPd`), its body applied by the era's BBODY, and the mirror rebuilt with the tip,
  the new-epoch state, the chain-dependent state and the node's bookkeeping (`MirrorRules`, `MirrorStep`). Nothing is
  converted between the node's flat state and the mirror while it runs. `--rules old` and `--rules differential` stay
  until J2.9 on the flat `NodeState`, unchanged (`Main` is generic over the form of the state, `Mode[S]`). The mempool
  (`MempoolLedger`, `Sts(Ledger)`; `Rejection.NoLedger` while the chain has no Conway ledger), `BlockBuilder.select` and the
  `Forger` (`Hfc.tick` to the slot, the leader schedule from `nesPd`) run on the Conway ledger of the new-epoch state, read
  through `ConwayView` over `ChainRead`. Design: `docs/design/ledger-mirror-step2.md` section 4, item 8.
- **The first block of each era is applied by the new rules** (the gap J2.5 left): the mirror's two telescopes are extended
  into the era the tick translated the state to (`MirrorStep.into`, the combinator's `Telescope.extend`; the translations are
  `translateLedgerState*Wrapper` and the era's `TranslateEra NewEpochState`, cited on `MirrorStep`), the Byron prefix is the
  Byron rules' on the mirror's Byron state, and the hand-over is the reference's translation built from the Shelley genesis
  (`NodeSetup.handOver`). `DifferentialErasSuite` applies 680 of 681 Shelley-based blocks (was 675), and `MirrorRulesSuite`
  runs the production rules over the whole eras capture beside the old rules, 731 of 731 blocks, with the new-epoch state, the
  header state, the consensus bookkeeping and the telescope bounds equal. A ledger of another era than the block's is the typed
  `BlockFailure.LedgerEra`.
- **The redeem outputs of the entry into Allegra are carried, not fetched.** The hand-over stashes them in the new-epoch state,
  `Translate.shelleyToAllegra` removes them with `Utxo.removeKnown` (the store holds the outputs out of the state's diff, which
  `--` cannot see), and the set's stamp is the block's number before the tick (a deletion stamped with the previous block never
  reaches the store, the B24 hazard). `AllegraRedeemMirrorSuite` is `AllegraRedeemStoreSuite` on the new rules over a store
  and fails without the stamp.
- **Snapshots: format 4, unchanged, read and written as the mirror** (`SnapshotV4.mirrorFormat`). No new version: the sidecar,
  its fingerprint (`CodecSuite`) and the Haskell section are those of J1.5. The sidecar's whole parameter sets are put back
  on the read (the section has an era's fields only, and the translations rely on the later eras' being there), the future
  ones laid over with what the update changed (`ProtocolParams.overlay`). A file the old rules wrote is restored once as a state
  of the new rules (`LegacySeed`: the reward update attributed, the pulser's dropped fields, `mark` as the snapshot was taken
  where the old writer read the accounts as they were, the old bookkeeping reset); the start state goes through it too.
  `UtxoBacking` is over any state with a `UtxoHandle`. `SnapshotMirrorSuite` writes and reads back every state of the eras
  capture whole, and reads the flat form's files. A database that the new rules extended is not for `--rules old`.
- **The VRF key-hash map is the reference's from the flip** (finding J2.6-1; the K1 projection does not include it).
- **`--mirror-check` applies to the flat state** (`old`, `differential`) and is ignored under the default; `devnet.py
  soak --mirror-check` passes it only there. `RulesMode`'s default is `New`; `devnet.py --rules` help says so.

### Fixed (J2.8, found by the devnet `e2e` on the default rules)
- **The mirror's reading of the reward update summed every reward of a credential**, where the reference pays the least before
  Allegra (`aggregateRewards`, by the previous parameters: the update of the first Allegra epoch is applied by version 2's
  rule). The flat state had held the filtered amounts, so the readers (the projection and the ledger-state comparison of the
  devnet) differed from the reference in the first Allegra epoch of a chain that has a credential with several rewards.
  `MirrorEraRead.rewardsDue` applies `Rewards.aggregate` (`RewardsDueSuite`).
