### Added (era types, #85)

- Alonzo's Plutus side has era types of its own: Alonzo has its UTxO classes (the scripts needed and provided, the keys that must sign, the datums and the annotated transaction) and its Plutus V1 script context, which leaves an input or output at a Byron address out rather than refusing it, as the reference does at Alonzo. They give what the node's current path gives on the Alonzo transactions of the context matrix (whose retained rows are the v1-context corpus's), the refusal corpora and the forecast-horizon corpus with the outputs they spend, and on the recorded blocks and Plutus admission corpora with stand-in outputs (whose spending scripts and datums are therefore not compared). Every Alonzo script context the reference recorded collecting is matched byte for byte. Nothing in the node uses these types yet, so behaviour and the persisted format do not change.
