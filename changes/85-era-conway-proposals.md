### Added (era types, #85)

- Conway's governance proposals and votes have era types of their own (`ConwayProposalProcedure`, `ConwayGovAction`, `ConwayVotingProcedures`), selected by era (`ProposalProcedure[E]`, `GovAction[E]`, `VotingProcedures[E]`) as the reference's `ProposalProcedure era` is. A proposal's action is at the reference's types: a parameter change carries Conway's update (`PParamsUpdate[Conway]`), each parent is the id of its own purpose (`GovPurposeId`), and the committee, epochs, threshold, version and script hashes are typed. Conway's body and its proposing purpose carry them. Every recorded Conway proposal, of each of the seven actions, reads with the fields the existing decoder reads, and what the typed proposal would refuse the existing decoder refuses first. No rule uses them yet, so behaviour does not change.
