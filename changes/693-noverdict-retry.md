### Changed (chain selection, #693)

- A block the node has no verdict on for a cause that can pass on its own (an exception of the rules, a crypto provider's failure, the evaluator's defect or missing result) is judged again: 1 s after the first attempt, the wait doubled after each attempt up to 30 s, measured from the end of an attempt, with no limit on attempts, until a verdict (the block is judged valid, or recorded as invalid with no peer charged), pruning at the immutable cutoff or a restart. A deterministic cause (a builtin, costing function or budget this build lacks, the evaluator's stack, a state quantity out of range) is still judged once. Only the selection worker's reselection retries, one due block at a time (with the withheld blocks of its chain) behind the queued admissions; a delivery, a reconnect or an admission neither resets nor advances the schedule. An approved amendment of the #567 deviation (ADR 0017, 2026-11-02).
- `Chain.BlockNoVerdict` carries `attempt` and `retryInMs`; the new `Chain.BlockNoVerdictRetry` line and the `scarab_block_no_verdict_retries_total` counter count each attempt after the first, and `scarab_block_no_verdict_total` counts each block once. The stall report (`Chain.StallState`) lists the withheld blocks (`withheld`: hash, slot, kind, attempts, next attempt).
