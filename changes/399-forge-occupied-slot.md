### Changed (block production, #399)

- When the tip already holds a slot the pool leads, forge an alternative on that block's predecessor (same height), as the reference's `forkBlockForging` does, instead of skipping the slot. Chain selection decides between the two blocks as it does for any slot battle. The alternative is not forged when that predecessor is older than the stability window, the bound already applied to the tip, as the reference forecasts the ledger view from the block's predecessor. After a boundary block, the block forged in its slot is its successor, as in the reference. A tip in a later slot is still skipped, now counted as `blockFromFuture` (the reference's `TraceBlockFromFuture`), and an immutable tip in or after the slot as `slotIsImmutable` (`TraceSlotIsImmutable`); the single `slotOccupied` reason, label `SlotOccupied` in `scarab_forge_skipped_total`, is replaced by these two.
