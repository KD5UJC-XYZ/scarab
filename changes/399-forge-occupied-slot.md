### Changed (block production, #399)

- When the tip already holds a slot the pool leads, forge an alternative on that block's predecessor (same height), as the reference's `forkBlockForging` does, instead of skipping the slot. Chain selection decides between the two blocks as it does for any slot battle. After a boundary block, the block forged in its slot is its successor, as in the reference. A tip in a later slot is still skipped, now counted as `blockFromFuture` (the reference's `TraceBlockFromFuture`), and an immutable tip in or after the slot as `slotIsImmutable` (`TraceSlotIsImmutable`); the single `slotOccupied` reason, label `SlotOccupied` in `scarab_forge_skipped_total`, is replaced by these two.
