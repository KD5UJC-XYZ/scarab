### Fixed (network)

- The node's mini-protocol decoders resume from where they stopped on the part of a message already held, as the reference's driver feeds each chunk to its decoder's continuation, instead of decoding the whole message again from its start after each SDU. The parts each keeps are the ones the tests pace a byte or a field an SDU: TxSubmission2's transactions, ids and offers; a handshake's terms and string chunks; PeerSharing's addresses; ChainSync's header heads, payload and points; LocalStateQuery's query heads, detached records and envelopes, which it no longer copies while the query is incomplete; and LocalTxMonitor's ids and measures. Messages are accepted and refused as the previous decoders did, at the same byte, which a comparison with those decoders checks on a corpus of each codec's messages, with mutations and truncations: every prefix up to 1,500 bytes and sampled cuts of longer ones; limits are unchanged. The Leios clients of the hand-run Leios probe still read each prefix from its start.
- The ingress queue counts the payload bytes of the SDUs it holds against its limit, as the reference's does; the heap each queued SDU takes beside its payload has not been measured against the reference's.
