### Added (era types, #85)

- Babbage's Plutus side has era types of its own: the script class builds and reads Babbage's purposes as the reference's does, and Babbage has its UTxO classes (the scripts needed and provided, the keys that must sign, the datums and the annotated transaction) and its Plutus V1 and V2 script contexts. They give what the node's current path gives on the Babbage transactions of the recorded contexts, the context matrix and the forecast-horizon corpora with the outputs they spend, and on the recorded blocks, refusal corpora and Plutus admission corpora with stand-in outputs (whose spending scripts and datums are therefore not compared). Every Babbage script context the reference recorded collecting or refusing is matched byte for byte. Nothing in the node uses these types yet, so behaviour and the persisted format do not change.
