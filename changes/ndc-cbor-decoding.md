### Added (conformance tooling)

- `tools/runMain xyz.kd5ujc.scarab.ledger.conformance.CborDatasetMain <dataset/era> <conway|dijkstra> <dataset-commit> <scarab-commit> <report.json>` runs one era of the cardano-cbor-dataset through the ledger's production decoders and writes the version 2 report of node-diversity-conformance's `cbor-decoding` scenario. Each rule of the dataset is read by the decoder Scarab reads that structure with in a block, directly or inside a minimal parent, and a valid sample is written again by a production encoder (the Haskell-format codecs of the ledger state, the header body's encoding, `Tx.envelope`) or from the original bytes the decoded value keeps. A rule without a decoder, and a decoded value without an encoder or kept bytes, are reported as failures.
