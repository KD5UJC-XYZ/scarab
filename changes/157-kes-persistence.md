### Fixed (KES key persistence, #157)
- **KES write-back now completes short writes and verifies the staged key before erasing its predecessor.** The stage entry is directory-synced before predecessor overwrite; predecessor bytes are fully written and forced before atomic replacement, followed by a directory sync. Recovery and retries preserve a valid staged key across failures at each boundary. Filesystem and readback failures retain typed causes. The envelope and serialized key format are unchanged. Power-loss durability depends on filesystem/device guarantees, and overwrite does not guarantee physical erasure on copy-on-write or journaling storage.
