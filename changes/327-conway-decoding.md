### Fixed (ledger decoding, #327)

- Decode Conway transaction bodies and witness sets at the era's decoder version, as the reference does. From version 9 a repeated element of an input, collateral, required-signer, reference-input, certificate or proposal set, a repeated withdrawal account or voter, an empty optional collection (certificates, withdrawals, mint, collateral, required signers, reference inputs, votes, proposals), a zero donation, key 6, an empty witness list and a Plutus script listed twice are refused at decoding. Repeated vkeys, native scripts, datums and redeemer keys stay accepted, as in the reference. Tag 258 and the map form of redeemers are read only from the versions that read them.
- Judge the certificate and proposal sets on what the reference's derived `Ord` compares, not on Scarab's stored encodings: two pool registrations that differ only in how their relays, metadata or margin are written, and two proposals that differ only in the order or encoding of a parameter update, of treasury withdrawals or of a committee update, are one element and a repeat is refused.
- Read a rational as the reference does below decoder version 12: tag 30 over two integers of any sign or size (bignums included), reduced, then bounded to the field's interval. A pool margin or a committee quorum written `30([-1, -2])` is one half, as in the reference, and one out of 0 to 1 is refused; repeated proposals and registrations are found whatever signs or integer forms their rationals use.
- Read a bignum inside a rational only from the one-byte tag heads over a definite byte string, as cborg's `decodeInteger` does; read `rho`, `tau` and the voting thresholds of a parameter update translated for scripts as unit-interval rationals; a pending update outside its interval is no longer adopted (`docs/compatibility.md`, ADR 0006 continuation).
