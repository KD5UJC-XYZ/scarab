### Added (crypto, #1418)

- Two differential fuzz targets, run by `check`, the nightly fuzz campaign and the pull-request fuzz gate.
  `curve25519-arithmetic` drives the comparisons of `FieldElementSuite`, `ScalarSuite` and `EdwardsPointSuite` from
  fuzzer-chosen operands: the vendored field, scalar and point arithmetic against exact integer arithmetic and the
  RFC 8032 model, and the scalar multiplications (constant-time, basepoint table, variable-time, and the
  verification kernel's) against each other and the model, with every returned point's extended coordinates
  checked. `ed25519-verification` compares `StrictEd25519` and `ByronEd25519` with the JDK's and Bouncy Castle's
  Ed25519 on triples as given, valid signatures mutated and the cross-check suite's edge families, and fails only on
  a disagreement outside the documented differences between their rules. No dependency is added: Bouncy Castle is
  already one of `crypto`'s.
