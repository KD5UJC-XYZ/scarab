### Fixed (devnet ledger oracle, #91)

- `devnet.py e2e --oracle`, `soak --oracle` and `oracle` compare an epoch-boundary digest only when its block, identified by header hash, is on both final chains. These are reference node 1's ImmutableDB and the follower's chain. The follower's chain is rebuilt from its whole log, one session from Origin per start, with every event linked to the tip. It is then checked against the follower's final tip, which is recorded while its peer, reference node 1, is stopped. A log that does not describe one chain fails the comparison. A digest of a block that a one-block fork rolled back is no longer compared with the state of another block.
- Every other digest is excluded with a reason: `rolledBack`, `notFinalOnFollower`, `notYetImmutable` or `rolledBackOnFollower`. The summary counts compared, matched and excluded digests and lists the epochs left without a matched digest.
- A run fails on any difference or error, when the immutable chains of the two sides disagree, on a digest whose block the follower's log never extended, and when no digest is compared and matched.
- Each digest file is now its own row. Two digests at one slot used to overwrite each other.
