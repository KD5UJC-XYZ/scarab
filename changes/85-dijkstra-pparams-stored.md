### Added (ledger, #85)

- Dijkstra's parameter record is read from and written back to the parameters the node keeps (`DijkstraPParams.fromStored` and `toStored`), and built from a decoded Dijkstra update (`fromUpdate`), as Conway's is. Parameters without Dijkstra's fields, which hold zero where Dijkstra requires a non-zero stride and positive ratios, are refused rather than read. `DijkstraPParams.downgrade` mirrors the reference's `downgradeDijkstraPParams` (cardano-ledger 9df9f98c). Every update of the Leios build's parameter corpus that the decoder reads, applied through the record, gives the parameters that the node's own update of the flat record gives.
