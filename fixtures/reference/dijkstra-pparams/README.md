# Dijkstra protocol-parameter updates

167 updates measured on 2026-11-21 against the unchanged Haskell ledger of the public Leios testnet build
(cardano-node `b004fe0c`, cardano-ledger `9df9f98c`, cardano-ledger-dijkstra 0.5.0.0), for
[#85](https://github.com/KD5UJC-XYZ/scarab/issues/85). The oracle source and
[recipe](../../../scripts/oracle/dijkstra-pparams/README.md) are committed; `provenance.json` records the
image identity and the program's hash. Raw run artifacts are not published.

`cases.jsonl` holds one case per line:
- `id`: the case's name;
- `update`: the hex of a `PParamsUpdate DijkstraEra` of version 12, a map of update keys to values;
- `reference`: either the decoder's refusal as `show` renders it (`refused`), or what the build makes of
  the decoded update:
  - `ppuWellFormed` at versions 12.0 and 10.0 (`wellFormed12`, `wellFormed10`;
    `eras/dijkstra/impl/src/Cardano/Ledger/Dijkstra/PParams.hs:1149-1175`);
  - `modifiedPPGroups` (`groups`), each group a DRep group and a stake-pool group;
  - the decoded update as the build writes it, `serialize'` at version 12 (`reencoded`;
    `EncCBOR (PParamsUpdate era)`, `libs/cardano-ledger-core/src/Cardano/Ledger/Core/PParams.hs:250-262`).

The cases:
- the empty update;
- every update key alone, with a zero value and a non-zero one;
- every field set, then every field set but a zero `nOpt` or a zero `eMax`;
- two mixed updates;
- an unknown key;
- cost models on both sides of the reference's `lengthThreshold` of 23 (`Encoding/Encoder.hs:442-468`): maps of
  23, 24 and 30 languages, lists of 23, 24 and 200 parameters (Int64's bounds among them), of a known and an
  unknown language, and 24 languages of 24 parameters;
- updates the build accepts but does not write so (non-canonical): indefinite maps and lists where it writes
  definite ones (the update, the cost models, a cost model, a ratio, the prices, the execution units and both
  threshold records), wide heads of a key, a value, a coin and a ratio's tag and items, keys and languages out
  of order, and an unreduced ratio;
- updates the build refuses, most with two faults, so that the fault it names first is its verdict: ratios of
  one or three items with a bad item or a zero denominator, a negative item, cost models of a repeated language
  with a bad list after it, a language past `Word8`, a negative language, a bignum or a value past `Int64` as a
  parameter, execution units, prices and pool thresholds of an indefinite list that ends before a field,
  records of too many items or a null first, a definite record of too few items before another key and at the
  end of the update, a repeated key with a bad value, a bad value before an unknown key, a negative word and a
  `Word32` past its width.

The build refuses 31: a zero reference-script cost stride (a `NonZero Word32`), a zero reference-script cost
multiplier and a zero Peras healing factor (each a `PositiveInterval`), the unknown key, and the 27 malformed
updates. In every case the verdict is the same at versions 12.0 and 10.0: Dijkstra's well-formedness does not read
the version. The build writes back the bytes of 113 of the 136 it accepts. The other 23 are the 6 that hold a definite list or
map past 23 items, which it writes indefinite, and 17 of the non-canonical updates, which it writes canonical; the
other 2 are indefinite past 23 items, as it writes them.

`DijkstraPParamsSuite` (ledger) decodes each update with the node's flat decoder and compares the verdicts, the
well-formedness at both versions and the groups. `EraPParamsCborSuite` (ledger) decodes each with the era codec,
compares each refusal's typed cause with the fault the build names, and writes each accepted update back to the
build's `reencoded` bytes. Not measured: applying an update to parameters, the parameters' own encoding, and any
decoder version below 12.
